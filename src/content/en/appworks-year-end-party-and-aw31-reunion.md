Jan 9 was AppWorks' Mafia Year-End Party.
But honestly, the real event for me started at lunch.

We (AW#31) took the chance to organize our own reunion first.
11 of us showed up: Ramos Chang, CHUN FENG LIU, Jon, Mabel Loh, KaKit Chen, Wei-Kang Chi, Hsi-Chun Tsai, Chih Yao Hu and more.

Just eating, talking, catching up, and sharing where life has taken us.
We started at 12:30, talked until 17:30, then walked together to the Year-End Party 🍺

Thanks to everyone who came from different places, and thanks to AppWorks and Jamie Lin for sponsoring lunch.

The night was packed, people everywhere, lots of familiar faces. Jung-Hong Kim and David Low were there too.

I won NT$2,000 in the lucky draw.
Honestly, I was still hoping for the vacuum cleaner. 🤣

Lucky to still be here with people who get it.
