First anniversary event

Last Saturday I went to the first anniversary meetup hosted by Twinkle AI and GDG. It was basically one giant internet-friends meetup. I'd seen a lot of these people online and only knew them by their IDs, so when I met them in person I couldn't actually recognize them 🤣

Big thanks to Liang-Hsun Huang 🧠🧪 for inviting me and giving me the chance to introduce AILogora to everyone. There were more than 70 people there and it was really lively. I met a lot of very cool people, and the food and drinks were amazing too!

Without really noticing, I've been lurking in TwinkleAI for almost a year now. Watching everyone put unpaid work into Traditional Chinese open source, I've felt firsthand that self-driven passion of the open source ecosystem, the kind that builds up over the long run. They've also released quite a few interesting open source models lately, and I'd recommend keeping an eye on them.

The only downside I have to mention is....
I ate three slices of that star (cult) cake 🫠
